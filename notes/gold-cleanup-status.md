## Architecture (the rule for all of this work)

`data/citations.tsv` is the **faithful raw extraction** and is never hand-edited
(it is regenerated by `pipeline/extract_wide.js` from the frozen xlsx). All
cleanup is **downstream curation**: corrections/exclusions applied in
`pipeline/build_gold.js`, duplicate removal via a drop-list sidecar. Everything
is reversible and reproducible. This mirrors the existing `nivel de` precedent.
