# Cryptoclass indices for the Spanish emonyms (ПоКА · ПА · ИРа · СИ) — GOLD basis

Generated by `pipeline/compute_indices.js --gold` from the five frozen gold sets `data/derived/gold-<emonym>.tsv` (GOLD basis, N≈9984) and the
vetted classifier inventory. Base-lemma counting (option 1). `nivel de` and
`círculo de` excluded class-wide. Support verbs (coger/traer/…) kept.

- **ПоКА** = Sᵢ/Σ_cls Sᵢ — depth (the emonym's mass on this class, normalized across the name's own projections; row sums ≈100%)
- **ПА** = Sᵢ/Σ_em Sᵢ — relative activity (the emonym's share of the class mass among the sample's names; column sums = 1.000)
- **ИРа** = Qᵢ/M — breadth (share of the class's classifier inventory reached)
- **СИ** (сочетательная избирательность) = the cᵢⱼ profile — which classifiers and how often (quantity + quality), reported in the profile section below. The "lead-classifier share" only summarizes its concentration; it is NOT СИ itself.
